Our Process

Built Around Proper Preparation.

Our step-by-step installation process ensures your garage floor is properly prepared, coated, and protected for long-term durability, performance, and a clean finished look.

01

Consultation & Estimate

We begin with an on-site visit to inspect the concrete, review color options, and provide a clear estimate.

02

Garage Preparation

To help us work efficiently, the garage floor is fully cleared for complete access during installation.

03

Surface Grinding & Repair

We grind the concrete and repair cracks to create a strong, properly prepared surface for adhesion.

04

Base Coat Installation

A base coat is applied to seal the concrete surface and strengthen adhesion for the next layer.

05

Full Flake Broadcast

We broadcast decorative flakes across the surface to enhance durability and achieve a uniform finish.

06

Protective Wearcoat

We apply a durable polyaspartic or polyurethane protective topcoat to seal the surface, enhance gloss, and protect against stains, scratches, UV damage and daily wear.
